Topics and submissions

We invite foundational research, prototypes, measurements, and deployment studies on data, computing, communication, and sensing infrastructure for autonomous physical systems, including AI/ML infrastructure, systems integration, orchestration, interoperability, dependability, and testbeds. Submit up to six pages, including references, in IEEE double-column format. Review is single-blind. Include author names and affiliations. Accepted papers appear in the IEEE MOST 2027 proceedings and are submitted to IEEE Xplore.
